Our Story

From a Liquor Market
to Phoenix’s Lounge

Plan a Visit

24th Street Cigar Lounge began with humble beginnings as a liquor market — and grew, stick by stick and pour by pour, into a premier lounge at the corner of 24th Street and Osborn.

Co-owned by George Janko, the lounge is proudly family-run. That is the whole idea here: walk in as a guest, settle in like family, and let the evening take its time.

“Here, George Janko welcomes you to be a part of our tapestry.”

— From the lounge’s own story
