01 — The Lounge

One room. Every kind of night.

It started as a neighborhood liquor market. Now it is Phoenix's cigar room — humidor up front, full bar in the middle, patio out back.

The Humidor

300 cigar options, from light bodied to full, from all around the globe. Ask at the counter — the crew will find your smoke.

The Bar

50 different premium spirits and craft cocktails. A full bar, poured right, steps from your chair.

The Patio

Outdoor seating out back for intimate gatherings and desert evenings. Some nights, it is the best seat in the city.
